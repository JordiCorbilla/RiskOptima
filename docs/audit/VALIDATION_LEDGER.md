Monte Carlo tolerances are several standard errors for the fixed 500,000-path fixture. A second deterministic test fixes the simulated counts and shocks to isolate the required square-root scaling from sampling error. The mixture uses a Poisson mean of 5 and truncated probability beyond 39 is negligible at the stated tolerance. IV round trips range over sigma from 10% to 65%, maturities from 0.35 to 3 years, and both option sides. No claim extends to stress parameters outside these fixtures.
